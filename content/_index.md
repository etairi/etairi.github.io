I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at [UC Berkeley](https://www.berkeley.edu/), hosted by [Sanjam Garg](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~sanjamg/). Prior to this, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at [CASCADE Team](https://crypto.di.ens.fr) of [ENS Paris](https://www.ens.fr), hosted by [David Pointcheval](https://www.di.ens.fr/david.pointcheval). I completed my PhD at [Security & Privacy Research Unit](https://secpriv.wien/) of [TU Wien](https://www.tuwien.at/) under the supervision of [Matteo Maffei](https://informatics.tuwien.ac.at/people/matteo-maffei) and [Daniel Slamanig](https://danielslamanig.info/). I was fortunate to be awarded an Erwin Schrödinger Fellowship.
